How to fix
  checkbox-layout: Scope full-width input styles to text fields (`input:not([type=checkbox]):not([type=radio])`), give checkboxes `width: auto` (Tailwind `w-4 h-4 shrink-0`), and put the box and label in a row: `display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 8px` (or `items-start` for multi-line labels).
  dropdown-arrow: Chrome and Safari draw the native arrow near the border and ignore padding-right. Use `appearance: none` with your own chevron, e.g. `background: url(chevron.svg) no-repeat right 12px center; padding-right: 40px`, or position an icon inside the padding. Keep the arrow's right inset close to the text's left inset.
  input-icon-overlap: Add padding on the icon's side so the text starts after it: e.g. `padding-left: 40px` (Tailwind `pl-10`) for a 16px icon at `left: 12px`, or `pr-10` for a trailing icon.
